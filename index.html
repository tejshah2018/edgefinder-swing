How rotation and flow are calculated

This is a transparent relative-strength chart, not proprietary JdK RRG. Weekly ETF/SPY adjusted-close ratios are compared with their trailing 13-week average: x = 100 × (ratio / average − 1). Momentum (y) is the four-week change in x. The current partial week is included; the earlier zone is four weekly observations back.

Flow score is 20-session Chaikin Money Flow: sum of [(2 × close − high − low) / (high − low)] × volume, divided by volume. A zero-range day contributes zero pressure. Buying pressure begins at +0.05; selling pressure begins at −0.05. Negative flow with positive relative trend is labeled selling into strength.

Dollar pressure sums that multiplier × volume × adjusted close over 20 sessions. It is a signed trading-pressure estimate, NOT actual ETF inflows, outflows, creations or redemptions. It is not comparable to reported fund-flow dollars. No actual net-flow dataset is connected.

One month = 21 aligned trading sessions; three months = 63. Relative return is ETF return minus SPY return in percentage points. YTD uses the final available prior-year close. Adjusted prices include provider corporate-action/distribution adjustments. Missing or stale funds are excluded from the chart and summaries.

RESEARCH MODEL

Edge checklist + historical test

EOD / 10 bars

Factor weights: Trend 25 · Momentum 20 · Relative strength 20 · Volume / closing pressure 15 · Structure 10 · Risk 10.

A confirmed trade also requires: aligned 20/50/200 EMAs, positive directional 20-day return, stronger/weaker performance than SPY, matching SPY regime, breakout close, relative volume ≥1.1×, risk ≤10%, and non-stretched RSI. Mixed SPY regime blocks new triggers.

After a scan is loaded, run the test on available historical daily bars. Signal at close, next-session open entry, 2R/stop/10-session exit. Test is illustrative, not live-validated. Holds beyond 10 sessions, up to 2–3 weeks, require a separate discretionary review and are not modeled in this test.
Data coverage & limitations

Signals use adjusted historical daily OHLCV from an unofficial provider. Some symbols may be missing, especially recent listings and macro series. Historical results omit slippage, commissions, borrowing costs and overlapping-position capital limits. All exits use daily OHLC, with stop prioritized if stop and target hit on the same session. The strategy has not been independently validated for profitability.

For new permanent symbols, edit config/watchlist.json in the GitHub repository and run the workflow. Personal browser filters do not modify the server universe.
